1. Getting started

MyFamTreeCollab runs entirely in your browser. No installation needed — just open the website and start. Your data is stored locally on your device by default.

Demo mode: If you open the app without an account, load the demo family tree — it contains two families (De Vries and Martens) across multiple generations. You can try out all features this way; demo data is not saved. Create a free account to build and save your own family tree.

The navigation bar at the top takes you to all sections: Manage, Family Tree, Timeline, Statistics and Export.

2. Creating an account and logging in

An account gives you access to local storage and cloud synchronisation across devices. Registration is free. There are two account types:

  • Basic account guest — free. Local family tree up to 60 people. No cloud, no collaboration. All new accounts start as basic accounts.
  • Owner account owner — via a donation (~€5 per 6 months). Unlimited people, cloud backup, sharing family trees and inviting others.
💡 Viewer and Editor are not account types. They are family tree permissions that an Owner grants for a specific tree. See section 16 for more details.

Creating an account

  • Click Login in the bar at the top of the page.
  • Click the "Create account" tab.
  • Enter your username, email address and password.
  • Click "Create account".
  • You will receive a confirmation email — click the link to activate your account.
  • You can then log in. Your account starts as a Basic account.

Logging in

  • Click Login in the bar at the top.
  • Enter your email address and password.
  • Click "Log in".
  • Your username will appear at the top of the page.

Logging out

Click your username at the top to open the user menu, then click "🚪 Log out".

User menu

  • ⚙️ My Account — profile, password, cloud family trees
  • 📜 Version history — view and restore earlier versions
  • 💬 Collaborate — message board. Red number = unread messages.
  • 🚪 Log out — ends your session and clears local data

Notification badge

A red number appears next to your username when new messages are posted in family trees you have access to. It disappears automatically when you visit the Collaborate page.

3. Adding your first person

Every family tree starts with one person — the Main ID. This is usually yourself or the person whose family tree you are building.

  • Go to Start Now on the homepage, or click Manage in the navigation.
  • Click "New family tree" if you have no people yet.
  • Fill in the details: First name and Last name are required, the rest is optional.
  • Click "Save".
  • Check the details in the preview panel.
  • Click "Confirm & go to Manage".
Tip: You can always edit details later via the Manage page.

4. Managing your family tree

On the Manage page you add family members, edit existing people and record relationships.

Adding a person

  • Go to Manage in the navigation.
  • Click "+ Add".
  • Fill in the details. FatherID, MotherID and PartnerID link the person to others.
  • Click "Save".

Editing a person

  • Search for the person using the search bar at the top.
  • The table shows the selected person with their family.
  • Edit the details directly and click "Save".

Deleting a person

  • Search for the person using the search bar.
  • Click the 🗑️ icon on the left of the row.
  • Confirm the deletion. The ID is automatically removed from all relationships.

Recording relationships

FatherID, MotherID and PartnerID support multiple values. Each cell shows a dynamic list of input fields.

  • FatherID — one or more fathers (e.g. adoption, stepfather)
  • MotherID — one or more mothers
  • PartnerID — one or more partners

Adding and removing IDs

  • Click "+ ID" below a field to add an extra input.
  • Click "×" next to an input to remove that ID.
  • The last field is cleared rather than removed.
  • Click "Save" to store your changes.
Storage: Values are stored internally as P-001|P-003 — the widget handles this automatically.

Portrait photos for people

The Portrait column on the Manage page links a photo to an existing person ID, not to an event. Click the bust icon to add or view a portrait.

  • Grey bust: no portrait is linked; click to select a photo.
  • Green bust: a portrait is linked; click to load and view or replace it.
  • Red bust: the linked photo could not be loaded when opened.

Uploads reuse the owner's existing Cloudinary connection. The image URL is stored as an additional person property (PortretURL) against the existing ID. Photos are not preloaded: an image is fetched only when a green bust is clicked.

Testing status: the portrait extension has been prepared, but its behaviour on the local and published website has not yet been confirmed.

10. Version history

Required: Logged in as Owner.
  • Go to My Account → 📜 Version history.
  • Choose a family tree from the dropdown.
  • Click "👁 Preview" to view a version without loading it.
  • Click "⏪ Restore" to revert to a version.
  • Click "🔍 Compare" twice to view two versions side by side.

11. Cloud family trees

With an Owner account you can save multiple family trees to the cloud.

Required: Owner to save. Viewers/Editors can load shared trees via "🔗 Shared with me".

Saving a family tree

  • Go to Storage → ☁️ Cloud family trees.
  • Enter a name and click "➕ Save as new".

Loading a family tree

  • Go to Storage → ☁️ Cloud family trees.
  • Click "⬇️ Load" next to the desired tree.
  • The tree is now available locally and marked as ACTIVE.

Deleting a family tree

  • Click "🗑️ Delete" next to the tree.
  • Confirm the prompt. The ACTIVE bar disappears automatically.

Person data and Events

A cloud family tree stores person records (including the portrait PortretURL) and Events. Image files remain in Cloudinary; only their URLs are stored with the records. Loading a family tree makes both types of data available locally.

Resetting local person data only

Storage → My data → Reset family tree deletes only local person records. Local Events and data held in Supabase remain unchanged. The two Storage pages manage their own reset and loaded status. Reload the appropriate data before overwriting the cloud copy.

11A. Cloudinary

Cloudinary is used as external storage for images and documents that can be linked to an event as a source. The connection is an owner account setting and is available from the TopBar user menu.

  • Owner/admin can manage the Cloudinary connection.
  • One central Cloudinary configuration is used per owner account for all family trees owned by that account.
  • Only the Cloud name and an unsigned upload preset are stored.
  • API keys, API secrets, passwords and tokens must not be stored in this configuration.
  • When uploading from Event Manager, the direct secure_url returned by Cloudinary is stored as the source URL.
  • The upload logic uses the stable family-tree ID to separate files per tree.
  • An editor does not automatically receive the owner's Cloudinary upload capability.

Status v2.6.0: the new Cloudinary code and TopBar connection have been prepared. Final live behaviour still has to be verified after publication.

Portraits and Event sources

A portrait is linked to an existing person ID by the PortretURL field. Events can have Cloudinary links among their sources. These references are stored alongside the relevant person or Event records, without copying the image file into Supabase.

12. Events

Events let you record important occurrences linked to people in your family tree. Events are stored separately from person data locally, but remain connected to the same family tree through the existing person ID.

Birth and death

When Event Manager opens, the fixed base events for each person are checked and synchronised:

  • <person-ID>-001 — Birth
  • <person-ID>-002 — Death
  • <person-ID>-003 and higher — other events

Birth date and birth place are copied from the person's data to the Birth event. Death date and death place are synchronised in the same way with the Death event.

Managing events

  • Open Event Manager.
  • Search for and select the person whose events you want to view or add.
  • Existing events for that person are shown. If no regular event exists yet, you can add a new event using the next event ID.
  • Enter the event type, title, date, place, sources and notes, then save the change.

Event storage

Event Storage has three tabs:

  • 📋 My data — local events in this browser.
  • ☁️ Cloud events — events belonging to the same cloud family tree as the people.
  • 🔗 Shared with me — events from family trees shared with you.
Cloud link: People and events use the same cloud family tree. No separate event tree is created. A Viewer can load shared events; an Editor can also save the events of a shared family tree.

Deleting and publishing Events

In Event Manager, action buttons appear to the left of each Event. The bin button deletes an individual Event after confirmation. Fixed birth and death Events (-001 and -002) are excluded from this deletion action.

The eye button indicates publication status. Only the verified Owner of the active cloud family tree can publish or unpublish an Event. This action is unavailable without an active cloud tree. New Events are unpublished by default (published: false).

Privacy limitation: although the publication control is available in the interface, server-side protection for unpublished Events is not yet complete. Users with access to the shared Supabase tree may still be able to read unpublished Event data. Do not treat 'unpublished' as confidential storage yet.

Resetting local Events

Open Event Storage → My data → Reset events to clear only the local Events. Person records, portraits and cloud data are retained. Use Load to retrieve saved Events again. Note: opening Event Manager may recreate the fixed birth and death Events from person records.

Cloud Events and sharing

Event Storage → Cloud events lists family trees with their person and Event counts. The Shared with … or Not shared label reflects existing sharing permissions. Both Storage pages use the same invitations and roles; you do not need to invite someone twice. An invitation grants access to the family tree, not just its Events.

Save events to active tree updates only Event data. The combined cloud sync keeps person records including PortretURL and Events with the same cloud tree. After resetting local Events, load them again before overwriting anything in the cloud.

13. Importing

The import page provides three separate import options: family tree CSV/TXT, family tree JSON and Event JSON.

Importing a family tree via CSV/TXT

  • If needed, download the CSV template.
  • Go to Import via the navigation.
  • Choose a CSV or TXT file containing family tree data.
  • Click "Import".

During CSV/TXT import, duplicate person IDs are detected automatically. You can skip duplicates, overwrite existing people, import everything anyway, or cancel the import.

Importing a family tree via JSON

A family tree JSON file is intended as a complete backup. After confirmation, the current local family tree is replaced by the people in the JSON file. No merge or duplicate-choice dialog is used.

Importing events via JSON

An Event JSON file contains a complete backup of the local events. After validation and confirmation, the current local events are fully replaced by the events in the file. No merge is performed.

Note: Before a full JSON import, verify that you selected the correct family tree or Event file. Import is available only to Owner accounts.

14. Exporting

The export page lets you download the family tree and the events separately.

Exporting the family tree

  • CSV — export of the people in the family tree.
  • JSON — complete JSON backup of the people in the family tree.

Exporting events

Events are exported as one JSON file. This format also preserves participants, sources and the other event fields.

File names

The surname of the first person in the local family tree is used as the family name in downloaded file names:

  • <familyname>_stamboom_YYYYMMDD.csv
  • <familyname>_stamboom_YYYYMMDD.json
  • <familyname>_event_YYYYMMDD.json
Tip: Regularly keep both a family tree JSON and an Event JSON backup. This lets you restore people and events separately.

Sharing: the Owner informs the recipient

When sharing a family tree or its Events, no automatic invitation email is sent. The Owner grants Viewer or Editor access and informs the recipient personally. Both Storage pages use the same sharing permissions.

Automatic emails are limited to account sign-up confirmation and password recovery through Supabase Auth.

Note: hiding unpublished Events in the interface does not guarantee that the underlying cloud data is private on the server.

Inviting someone

  • Go to Storage → ☁️ Cloud family trees.
  • Click "🔗 Share" next to the tree.
  • Enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
  • Choose a permission: 👁 Viewer or ✏️ Editor.
  • Click "Invite".
Note: The invited user must already have an account. A free basic account is sufficient.

Revoking access

  • Open the share panel via "🔗 Share".
  • Click "Revoke" next to the user.

Viewing shared trees (Viewer/Editor)

  • Go to Storage → 🔗 Shared with me.
  • Click "⬇️ Load" to load the tree.

Sharing from either Storage page

Family Tree Storage and Event Storage both display and use the same sharing permissions for a cloud tree. Event Storage indicates whether the tree is shared; click Share to review or revoke existing Viewer or Editor access. Users already invited from Family Tree Storage must not be invited again.

Publication permissions: only the Owner may change an Event's publication status. An Editor's ability to save shared Events does not grant publication rights. Server-side protection of unpublished Events is not yet complete.

17. Message board (Collaboration)

The message board shows all family trees you have access to. Per tree you see discussions about specific people, organised by person ID.

Required: Logged in with access to at least one family tree.

Opening the message board

  • Click 💬 Collaborate in the navigation or in the user menu.
  • All trees you have access to are loaded, each in its own section.

Active vs. read-only trees

  • ✓ Active (blue accent line) — the loaded tree. You can start discussions and post messages here.
  • Read only — other trees. Click the link in the yellow banner to activate that tree.

Starting a discussion

  • Type a name or ID in the search bar of the active tree.
  • Click the desired person in the dropdown.
  • Confirm in the modal — a new discussion block appears.

Posting a message

  • Open a discussion block by clicking its header.
  • Type your message in the text field at the bottom.
  • Click "Send".

Discussion status

🔓 OpenActive and pending

Posting a change proposal (Editor/Owner)

  • Click "📝 Propose change" in the message form.
  • Describe the proposed change in the yellow text field.
  • Send the message — the proposal appears as a separate yellow block.

Deleting a message

Owners can delete any message via ✕. Viewers and Editors can only delete their own messages.
